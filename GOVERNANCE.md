# Governance

The repository owner appoints maintainers and has final responsibility for repository administration. Technical and policy claims are evaluated through public issues and pull requests.

Changes to preregistered study parameters require a new study version. Existing evidence is never silently reinterpreted under a changed specification. A policy recommendation requires published inputs, reproducible analysis, uncertainty disclosure, conflict disclosure, and documented maintainer review. External review is welcome and must be identified accurately, but it is not a completion dependency.

Maintainer status does not make a research claim authoritative. Evidence and reproducibility remain the basis for acceptance.
